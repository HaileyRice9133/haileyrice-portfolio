WHITE RABBIT  

Brand identity, composition, and product packaging.

White Rabbit is a tea brewery and retail brand with a passion for whimsical and flavourful blends with a youthful edge. Inspired by the fairytale, Alice in Wonderland, this company uses vibrant colours to reflect on the vibrant flavours of their blends.


The logo was composed of decorative text in a playful, psychedelic, colour palette of pale pastels and vibrant neons,and their signature bright pink and blue, comes together to create a recognizable brand. Their mascot is the white rabbit, derived from the old fashioned, original fairytales. He's known for holding a stopwatch, though these designs almost set back time to a completly unique era.


Beneath I have included various logos and 3D packaging mockups, including the English Afternoon tea signature box for individual tea bags, scaled to size.
