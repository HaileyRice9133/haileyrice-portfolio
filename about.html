ABOUT

AN ARTIST'S EYE,
A DESIGNER'S PRECISION.

Hi, I'm Hailey Rice, a graphic designer and artist from St. Thomas Ontario.

As a meticulous and detail oriented multidisciplinary designer, I combine my knowledge of the arts with the necessary technical skills to create highly functional designs, websites, and illustrations. My work is consistantly driven by storytelling, branding, colours, typography, and iconography that evokes emotion in the viewer, creating lasting association and a memorable identity.

Design has always been a passion of mine. At age fourteen, i started my first small business on Etsy selling hand-painted acrylic pet portraits on canvas, totebags, and phonecases. I learned about international shipping customs, along with the importance of designer to client communication skills. Now, with a plethora of creditble skills in graphic design -- and a set of certificates under my belt -- I am ready to create lasting, impactful designs that resonate with a variety of audiences.
