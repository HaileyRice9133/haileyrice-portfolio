GROOVELINE 

RECORDS 

Brand Identity, UI/UX Design, Mobile App Design, Advertising Campaign, Social Media and Retail Marketing.

Grooveline Records is a retail store and music discovery app that brings the record-store experience into the digital space. Users can shop new and pre-loved vinyl, receive personalized recommendations, track album availability, play purchased digital downloads, and sell qualifying records through the store. Designed for music lovers and collectors, Grooveline connects the thrill of finding a record in-store with the convenience of discovering and collecting music online.


When designing the main wordmark for Grooveline, I chose a font that had ample space inside the counters of the O's to place records, and had a visual baseline -- no pun intended -- to keep things cohesive. The curve of the L also represents the stem and flag of a music note. I then came up with a much smaller mark of simple initials, along with the record shaped motif that would work well for a badge or an app.


The colour palette was chosen to create an energetic and vibrant visual identity that reflects a modern take on vinyl culture. By combining the nostalgia of record collecting with the accessibility of a music discovery app, the brand bridges the gap between traditional and digital music experiences. The bright, electric tones reinforce a sense of community and exploration, helping position the retail store as both a shopping destination and a social hub for younger audiences. This approach creates a fresh and engaging brand that resonates with longtime collectors while introducing a new generation to the world of vinyl.
