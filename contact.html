FAQ

Do you understand accessibility and inclusive design?

Yes, I understand the importance of including alternate text for photos and web designs to ensure that all users can access my work barrier free.

Do you know how to receive successful printing results?

Yes. I understand the importance of setting up the document correctly from the start, and the process of using bleeds and crop marks. I am also familiar with printing on a variety of mediums with differing absorbencies, including newspaper.

Are you familiar with Adobe's AI services?

Yes, as a Graphic Designer in an evolving market, I am familiar with and able to utilize AI when necessary, though DO NOT rely on it alone. I have creativity for that!

While AI is a tool that can quicken a project's finish line — like removing backgrounds in Adobe Photoshop — it is important to understand how to use design tools both with and without it. This includes Adobe Firefly.

How does your experience with fine art transfer to graphic design?

Fine art has sharpened my eye to recognize colours, shapes, forms, and general hierarchy and harmony between graphics and their space. This meticulousness improves the integrity of my designs and successfully communicates with, and evokes feeling from, the viewer.
