GRAPHIC DESIGN FROM AN ARTIST'S PERSPECTIVE

Every creation begins with fundamental art & design principles. Guided by an eye for color & design theory, perspective, typography and illustration , I create meaningful, recognizable concepts into lasting impactful brand identities.

But great design must work as beautifully as it looks. By fusing my fine art roots with graphic design and UI/UX strategy , I translate artistic vision into intuitive digital interfaces, and functional 3D Blender models using your user experience to both visually striking and effortless to navigate.

ART & ILLUSTRATION

JESSE PINKMAN A painterly realism study over a vibrant red underpainting from a scene in Breaking Bad. It features Aaron Paul as Jesse Pinkman, captured from a sidewalk level perspective with detailed background.
BUS TO MUNICH Two fugitives are seated on a bus traveling to Munich. It's a snowy early morning, and despite the popularity of public transport, they are hoping to remain unnoticed.
STYLIZED PORTRAIT A portrait study of Sophie Thatcher as Natalie Scatorccio in the TV series Yellowjackets. This was a study with emphasis on light and form, and the planes of the face.
PEN TOOL, SELF PORTRAIT A self portrait created in Adobe Illustrator by laying shapes with the pen tool over a photo reference. Each shape needed to hold the colour fo the shadows below, to create depth and emhasize the form of my face, and the texture of fabrics along woth light and dark. It shows extensive skill with the pen tool, along with compositional knowledge.
